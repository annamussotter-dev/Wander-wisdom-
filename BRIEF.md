## 2. Zielgruppe

- Junge Reisende: Abiturient:innen, Gap-Year, Studierende vor dem Auslandssemester.
- **Moment der Suche:** kurz vor einer Reise oder einem neuen Lebensabschnitt.
- Auch als Geschenk für genau diese Menschen (Eltern, Freund:innen) – *Annahme, bitte bestätigen.*

## 3. Problem

- Man trifft unterwegs viele spannende Menschen, aber die Gespräche bleiben oberflächlich.
- Von einer Begegnung bleibt nichts Handfestes – höchstens ein Foto.
- Wer schon mal Leute in ein leeres Heft schreiben ließ, kennt das: ohne Fragen und Gestaltung
  kommen kaum gute Antworten, und alles klingt gleich.
- **Was verloren geht:** Erinnerungen, Kontakte, Austausch, neue Ideen und Perspektiven.

## 4. Was das Buch anders macht

- **Co-Creation:** Nicht du schreibst über die Leute – sie schreiben selbst, in ihrer Handschrift.
- **Gestaltete Fragen statt leerer Seiten:** Fragebögen mit je 3 tiefen Fragen, damit echte Antworten entstehen.
- **Mehr als Text:** Lieblingsorte (Hidden gems), Zeichnungen (Canvas of emotions), Doodles, Weltkarte.
- **Von einer Reisenden gemacht und unterwegs getestet** (erstes Exemplar in Australien).

### Problem / Concept

> **Along the way, it's the people we meet who make the greatest impact.**
>
> You'll meet hundreds of people on your travels. Most of them you'll remember as a face in a
> photo, if at all. The conversations stay small, and the best thing someone ever told you on a
> night bus is gone by the next morning.
>
> **There's a hidden gem in everyone – you just have to find it.**
> Wander Wisdom gives you the questions to find it, and a place to keep it.

### How it works (echte Reihenfolge – Nummerierung ist hier richtig)

> **How it works**
>
> 1. **Share the purpose.** Tell the person what the journal is for: capturing connections.
> 2. **Pick a questionnaire.** Choose one, let them choose, or make up your own.
> 3. **Give them time.** Good answers need a quiet moment, not a rushed one.
> 4. **Be respectful.** Everyone decides how much they want to share.
> 5. **Invite them further.** Ask for a hidden gem or a drawing on the canvas of emotions.
> 6. **Enjoy the journey.** Page by page, you build a collection of stories nobody else has.

### Was drin ist (Leistungen)

> **What's inside**
>
> **Your profile** – Before anyone else writes in it, the book gets to know you: your hometown,
> the principles you live by, one fear, your current goals, advice for your younger self and a
> message to your future self in five years.
>
> **Map to track** – A world map to mark where you've been and who you met there.
>
> **Questionnaires** – The heart of the book. Each one has three questions and space for the
> writer's name, hometown and socials. For example:
> – *Life is about…*
> – *Advice for people in their 20s*
> – *What makes a good friend?*
> – *What did travelling teach you?*
>
> **Quick connect** – For when time is short but the connection still matters: three people,
> three short answers on one page.
>
> **Create your own pages** – Write the questions only you would think of asking.
>
> **Hidden gems** – The people you meet share their favourite places, activities and artists.
> Your next itinerary, written by locals and fellow travellers.
>
> **Canvas of emotions** – *How does happiness feel to you? What does it look like?* People
> answer with a drawing. Art works across every language.
>
> **Doodle time** – Room for sketches, stickers, tickets and ideas.
>
> **20 prompts** – Questions for when you want to go deeper, like
> *What's the most valuable lesson you've learned from someone unexpected?*
