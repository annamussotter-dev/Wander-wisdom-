# WANDER WISDOM — book content

Text version of the printed journal (88 pages, A5-ish, made in Canva).
Use this as the source of truth for the website. The page images in this
folder show the visual style.

## Visual style of the book
- Bold black & white. Huge, heavy, condensed uppercase headlines
  (e.g. "CONCEPT", "PURPOSE", "HIDDEN GEMS", "CANVAS OF EMOTIONS").
- Clean, light geometric sans-serif for body text; italic for quotes.
- Dot-grid writing pages. Light-grey world-map silhouettes as section dividers.
- Thin grey corner-bracket frames. Very minimal, lots of white space.
- About page uses a dark charcoal background (#3a3938-ish) with white text.

## Concept
"There's a hidden gem in everyone – you just have to find it!"
As you journey through life, you meet unique individuals, each with their own
stories and perspectives. Through these connections you learn, you grow and you
find inspiration. But in today's fast-paced world we often miss the chance to
truly connect. Conversations are superficial and we capture memories only with
photos and videos.

## Purpose — "You will go beyond!"
By recording the wisdom of the people you meet, you hold onto more than fleeting
memories. The book exposes you to:
- great exchanges and special bonds
- new opportunities and ideas
- valuable perspectives from diverse cultures
- individual advantages
As people write their answers to themed questions, you build a living document
of your life's connections and lessons through your travels.

## Steps to your Wander Wisdom
1. Create your profile — give your book your personality
2. Map your adventure — track your journey and special encounters
3. Co-creation — questionnaires, hidden gems, canvas of emotions
4. Doodle — sketch, glue or draw to capture fleeting ideas
5. Inspiration — ideas and prompts for questions

### My path (how to use it with others)
The journal has 7 questionnaires, each with 3 thought-provoking questions,
marked with different shades of grey.
1. Share the purpose — introduce the journal and its goal of capturing connections
2. Pick a questionnaire — choose one, let the person decide, or create your own
3. Encourage reflection — invite thoughtful answers and give time
4. Be respectful — be gracious and respect their boundaries
5. Hidden gems & canvas of emotions — invite them to contribute to other chapters
6. Enjoy the journey — build a one-of-a-kind collection of stories and wisdom
Goal: uncover potential and meaningful moments, cutting through the superficial.
Tips: Be selective (choose people that feel meaningful). Take your time.
Stay open — every interaction holds value.

### The questionnaires (each page: location/date + name, hometown, socials)
- Life is about… / Advice to people who struggle to find purpose / A lesson from your travels that had great impact on you
- Principles you live by / Advice to form good & profound relationships / How can you help and inspire other people?
- Two important lessons from your travels / What brings you strength and joy for life / Advice for people in their 20s
- A message to Gen-Z / What would you tell your younger self? / What makes a good friend?
- Traveling is about… / What's a goal in your life and why? / What gives you strength and motivation?
- What did travelling teach you? / Specific qualities you value in people / Life is about…

## About the founder (back of book)
BY ANNA MUSSOTTER — wandering through life with curiosity & passion.
Three years ago, I carried a blank notebook and asked people to share their best
advice. What started as a simple idea quickly grew bigger. People connected with
the concept, opened up, and I realised the great value and uniqueness of
connecting that way. This sparked an idea: while studying for graduation, I began
designing WANDER WISDOM. Testing the first sample in Australia uncovered the most
beautiful insights from the people I met and led to unexpected, meaningful
moments that shaped my travels. Today, that first notebook is the most valuable
thing I own — and I hope this one becomes just as special for you.
